PrepMe

PrepMe is an AI meal planner for Egypt. Add what is in your kitchen, by hand or by scanning a receipt or a photo of your groceries, and PrepMe suggests recipes you can cook with it, in Arabic or English.

What it does

  • Keeps a pantry of what you have, and works offline.
  • Reads receipts and grocery photos to fill the pantry for you.
  • Suggests recipes and answers follow-up questions in a chat.
  • Subtracts what you cooked from the pantry, and shows roughly what you saved.

PrepMe is free to start. An optional Pro subscription raises the monthly limits. There are no ads and no tracking.

Legal and help

  • Privacy Policy – what we collect, who processes it, and how to delete it.
  • Terms of Use – subscriptions, AI-generated recipes, and your responsibilities.
  • Support – contact us, cancel a subscription, delete your account, export your data.

Allergies and AI. Recipes are written by AI and can be wrong. Always check ingredients and product labels yourself if you have an allergy or a medical condition.
