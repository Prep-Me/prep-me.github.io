Delete your PrepMe account

Effective date: 31 October 2026

This page explains how to delete your account in PrepMe, the pantry and recipe app for iPhone and Android published by Prep Me, and what happens to your data. Contact: prepme.business@gmail.com.

Option 1: in the app (immediate)

  1. Open PrepMe and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Privacy & receipt photos → Delete my account.
  3. Read the summary and confirm. If you signed in with Apple, you are asked to confirm with Apple so that the sign-in link can be revoked.

Your account and data are deleted right away.

Option 2: by email (if you cannot use the app)

  1. Send an email to prepme.business@gmail.com with the subject Delete my PrepMe account.
  2. Send it from the email address of your PrepMe account (the one shown when you signed in with Apple or Google), so that we can confirm the request comes from you. If you used Apple’s “Hide My Email”, write from the Apple ID email and tell us; we may ask a question to confirm it is you.
  3. We delete the account and reply to confirm, within 30 days.

Before you delete: cancel your subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel a Pro subscription. Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google Play, so cancel them there first (how). Otherwise you may still be charged.

What is deleted

  • Your account: sign-in link, email, name and profile.
  • Preferences, allergies, household members and your AI consent record.
  • Pantry items, chats, recipes and cooking history.
  • Scan results and any stored receipt or grocery photos.
  • Feedback you sent, devices, usage counts and usage events.
  • Our record of your subscription status.

What is kept, and for how long

  • AI call logs: technical records of AI requests (feature, model, token counts, time, cost, success) are kept for cost and abuse monitoring, without any link to you. They never contained your prompts, answers or photos.
  • Install ID and free-use counters: a random ID for the app installation, with its free-use counts and welcome-days record, without any link to you. Kept for abuse prevention (so a new account on the same phone does not get a new free allowance): counts for 3 months, the welcome-days record for 12 months.
  • Backups: copies may remain in our database provider’s backups for a short period before they are overwritten.
  • Data held by other companies under their own policies, which deleting PrepMe cannot remove: purchase and billing records at Apple, Google Play and RevenueCat; AI requests already sent to Anthropic, Google or Groq (see the Privacy Policy); and crash reports until Firebase expires them.

Want only some of your data deleted, or a copy first? You can delete items in the app, export your data from Profile → Privacy & receipt photos, or email us. See the Privacy Policy for details.
