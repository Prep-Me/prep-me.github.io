Support

Effective date: 22 October 2026

Need help with PrepMe? Write to us and we will reply as soon as we can.

Email: prepme.business@gmail.com
Please include your app version (Profile tab) and, if it is about a problem, what you were doing. Do not send passwords, and do not send photos of receipts or documents that show card numbers.

Cancel a subscription

PrepMe subscriptions are billed by Apple (iPhone) or Google Play (Android), so you cancel with them, not in PrepMe and not by deleting the app. In PrepMe you can open the cancel screen from Profile → Manage subscription.

iPhone

  1. On your iPhone, open Settings and tap your name at the top.
  2. Tap Subscriptions, then PrepMe.
  3. Tap Cancel Subscription and confirm.

Do this at least 24 hours before the renewal date, or you will be charged for the next period. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. For a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com; refunds are decided by Apple.

Android

  1. Open the Google Play app and tap your profile picture.
  2. Tap Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions, then PrepMe.
  3. Tap Cancel subscription and follow the steps.

Cancel before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds on Android are decided by Google Play.

Paid but still seeing Free? Open the upgrade screen and tap Restore purchases, check that you are signed in with the same account you bought with, and wait a minute. If it still shows Free, email us with your purchase date.

Delete your account

In the app, open Profile → Privacy & receipt photos → Delete my account and confirm. This permanently deletes your account, pantry, chats, recipes, allergies, scan results and stored scan photos. It does not cancel your subscription: cancel it first as above (the app reminds you and offers a Manage subscription button before you confirm). If you signed in with Apple, you are asked to confirm with Apple so that the sign-in link can be revoked. If you cannot open the app, email us from the address you signed up with and we will delete the account for you.

Export your data

In the app, open Profile → Privacy & receipt photos and choose the export option to save a copy of your data as a JSON file. You can also email us to ask for it.

A scan read something wrong

  • Nothing is added to your pantry until you review the list and confirm. Edit a name, quantity or unit, and remove lines that are wrong.
  • Photograph the receipt flat, in good light, with the whole receipt in frame, and no glare or folds. The app reads in your app language first and tries the other language if nothing is found.
  • If a scan fails or nothing could be read, retry with a clearer photo. A scan only counts toward your monthly limit when it succeeds.
  • You can always add items by hand in the Pantry tab.
  • Still wrong? Email us the app version and the time, without the photo if it has personal details.

Allergies and recipe safety

PrepMe uses the allergies you set when it suggests recipes, but AI can make mistakes. Always check ingredients and product labels yourself, and ask a medical professional about serious allergies. See the Terms.
