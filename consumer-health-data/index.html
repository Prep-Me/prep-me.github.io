Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 November 2026

This notice explains how PrepMe handles the allergy and other health-related information you give the app, and the privacy rights you have as a resident of Washington, Nevada, California or another US state with a privacy law. It adds to our Privacy Policy; where the two differ for this information, this notice applies. Contact: prepme.business@gmail.com.

The short version

  • PrepMe uses your allergies and foods to avoid for one purpose: to keep recipe ideas safe for you and your household.
  • In current versions of the app your allergy list is kept only on your phone. It is not sent to our servers or to our AI provider.
  • We do not sell this information, do not share it for advertising, and do not collect it from health apps, sensors, prescriptions or your location.
  • You can delete it, export it, or withdraw your consent at any time (section 5).

1. What health-related information we handle

  • Allergies and intolerances of yours and of the household members you add, chosen from the app’s list or typed as food names. Household members are never named to the AI.
  • Foods you would rather avoid and dietary preferences (for example halal), which the app treats as tastes and rules for recipes.
  • Goals you pick in setup, for example “eat healthier”. It is saved with your account preferences on our servers and is used only to tune the app’s suggestions.

We do not collect medical records, prescriptions, diagnoses, biometric data or precise location, and we do not try to work out whether you have a health condition.

2. Why we use it

Only to provide the service you asked for: leaving unsafe foods out of the recipes PrepMe suggests. The app checks every suggested recipe against your allergy list on your phone before it is shown, and hides a recipe that contains something on the list. We do not use this information for advertising, profiling, pricing, or any other purpose.

3. Where it is kept and who can see it

  • On your phone. In current versions your allergy list and your household’s lists are kept in the phone’s protected storage, are not part of our server database, and are removed from the phone when you sign out or delete your account. Accounts created before this change had a copy on our servers; the app deletes that copy when it moves the list onto your phone.
  • Our AI provider. The recipe request the app sends tells the server only that the allergy check is done on your phone; it does not contain your list. If you type an allergy yourself in Cook (for example “no peanuts”), those words are part of your request and go to our AI provider, Anthropic, in the United States, under the AI consent screen you agree to before first use. Under its commercial terms Anthropic does not train on this data and deletes it within 30 days, except where it must keep it longer to enforce its usage policies.
  • Our servers. Our database is in the European Union (Ireland) and holds your account. It holds your preferences, but not your allergy list once that has moved to your phone.

We do not sell consumer health data and do not share it with anyone for their own use. The only companies that handle it for us are the service providers named in the Privacy Policy, and only as described above.

4. Consent

The app asks for your agreement on its AI consent screen before your first AI or scan request, and asks again when this use changes. It sends nothing to the AI provider until you agree. We do not use geofencing around any place that provides health care.

5. Your rights

Whatever state you live in, you can:

  • Know what we hold about you and get a copy: in the app, Profile → Privacy & receipt photos, export option.
  • Delete it: in the app, Profile → Privacy & receipt photos → Delete my account, or at prepme-app.com/delete-account. You can also remove allergies one by one in Profile.
  • Correct it: edit your profile and household in the app.
  • Withdraw your consent to this collection and use at any time, by deleting your account or by writing to us. Without it the AI and scan features cannot be used.
  • Ask us which companies handle it for us (the list is in the Privacy Policy).

Write to prepme.business@gmail.com to use any right. We answer within 45 days, and may extend that once by another 45 days if we tell you why. We may need to confirm the request is from you. If we decline a request you can appeal by replying to our message within 60 days; if you are still not satisfied you can contact your state attorney general. Another person can send a request for you if you authorise them in writing.

6. Your privacy choices (California and other US states)

In the last 12 months PrepMe has not sold personal information and has not shared it for cross-context behavioural advertising. The app shows no ads. Because we do not sell or share personal information, and use sensitive information only to provide the service you ask for, there is nothing to opt out of or to limit. This website uses no cookies or trackers.

The categories of personal information we collect, why, and who handles them are in the Privacy Policy (sections 1 to 6). We do not discriminate against you for using any of these rights.

7. Children

PrepMe is for people aged 18 and over and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

8. Changes

If this notice changes in a way that affects your health information, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent again. We will update the effective date above.
