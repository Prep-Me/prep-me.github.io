Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

This policy explains what the PrepMe apps for iPhone and Android (“PrepMe”, “the app”) collect, why, who else handles it, how long we keep it, and what control you have. PrepMe (“we”, “us”) is the publisher and is responsible for your data. Contact: prepme.business@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You sign in with Apple or Google. We keep your email and name, plus what you add: pantry, recipes, follow-up requests, allergies and preferences.
  • Before you first use the AI and scan features, the app’s AI consent screen asks you to agree to how your data is sent to our AI provider, Anthropic (section 4). We keep a record of your agreement.
  • Receipt and grocery photos are uploaded, read by AI and then deleted, failed scans included. We never keep them.
  • Your recipe requests go through our own AI server to one AI provider: Anthropic (Claude), on its paid commercial service (section 4). Under its commercial terms, Anthropic does not train on this data and deletes it within 30 days, except where it must keep it longer to enforce its usage policies.
  • No ads. No tracking across other companies’ apps or sites. We do not sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and export your data inside the app.

2. AI recipes and follow-ups

The app asks for your agreement on its AI consent screen before your first AI or scan request, and asks again when this use changes. It sends nothing to the AI provider until you agree. You can withdraw your agreement by deleting your account or by writing to us; without it the AI and scan features cannot be used.

To write recipes and handle follow-ups, our Supabase server sends a request to our own AI server (section 4), which calls one AI provider: Anthropic (Claude), on its paid commercial service. Claude Haiku reads every request first. If it finds nothing usable in a scan photo, a larger Claude model (Claude Sonnet) may read that photo again. Both are run by Anthropic in the United States.

There is no free-text chat box in the app. The request can contain your pantry item names (with quantity and unit), the follow-up choices you tap (for example “something quicker” or “different dishes”), your preferences, and your allergies and those of the household members you added (as food names, never their names). Receipt and grocery photos are sent too (section 3). We do not send your name, email or account ID to Anthropic, and our AI server keeps no user accounts and no database.

While AI features are being switched on, some or all of them may at times answer with built-in sample content and send nothing to Anthropic.

Under its commercial terms, Anthropic does not train on this data and deletes it within 30 days, except where it must keep it longer to enforce its usage policies. AI output can be inaccurate; see the Terms.

We store the result in your account. Separately, we log technical details of each AI call (which feature, model, token counts, time taken, estimated cost and whether it succeeded) for cost control and debugging, and our AI server sends monitoring traces to our own self-hosted Langfuse with the content of prompts, pantry items, requests and recipes replaced by “[redacted]”. These logs and traces do not contain your prompts or the AI’s answers.

3. Receipt and grocery photos

  1. The app shrinks your photo and uploads it to a private storage area that only your account and our server can reach.
  2. Our server sends the photo, directly or through our AI server, to Anthropic. Claude Haiku reads it first; if it finds nothing usable, Claude Sonnet may read it again (section 2). There is no separate text-reading step. If Anthropic is not available, the scan does not complete and you can add the items by hand.
  3. The items it returns are shown to you to review before they are added to your pantry.
  4. The photo is deleted from our storage right after it is read, failed scans included. We keep the list of items, never the picture.

If you leave a scan halfway, any leftover upload is deleted automatically within 1 hour. Anthropic handles the photo under its commercial terms (section 2). Receipts can show names or partial card numbers, so please crop them out before scanning; do not scan anything else that is sensitive.

5. No ads, no tracking, no sale

PrepMe shows no ads and contains no advertising or cross-app tracking. We do not sell or rent your personal data and we do not share it for advertising. We may disclose data if the law requires it or to protect our rights and users’ safety.

Third-party services keep some data under their own rules, for example Anthropic for the AI requests it receives (section 2), and Apple or Google Play for purchases. Deleting your PrepMe account does not delete data those companies hold under their own policies; ask them directly if you want that.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account data (profile, allergies, household members, AI consent record, pantry, follow-up requests, recipes, cooking history, scan results, devices, usage events, feedback you sent, subscription record): until you delete your account. Items you delete in the app are marked deleted and removed with your account.
  • Scan photos: never kept. Deleted right after they are read, failed scans included; any leftover upload is deleted automatically within 1 hour.
  • AI requests at Anthropic: deleted by Anthropic within 30 days, except where it must keep them longer to enforce its usage policies.
  • AI call logs: technical details only, kept for cost and abuse monitoring. When you delete your account they are kept without any link to you.
  • AI server and its monitoring traces: our AI server keeps no user data. The traces in our own Langfuse are redacted technical details (steps, model, timings, token counts, errors) kept on our own server for monitoring.
  • Usage analytics: kept by Google Analytics for a limited period under our settings. They are not linked to your PrepMe account, so deleting the account does not remove them.
  • Crash reports: kept by Firebase for a limited period under its settings.
  • Billing records: kept by Apple or Google Play, and RevenueCat, under their policies and the law.
  • Backups: an encrypted copy of our database is made every day and kept for 30 days, then deleted. Only we hold the key. Data you delete stays in those copies until they expire.
  • Server logs: kept for a short period by Supabase and our AI server, then overwritten.

7. Your choices

Delete your account

In the app, open Profile → Privacy & receipt photos → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account and the data we hold for it: profile and preferences, allergies, household members, AI consent record, pantry, follow-up requests and recipes, cooking history, scan results, devices, usage events and our subscription record.

You can also ask for deletion without the app at https://prepme-app.com/delete-account/.

If you signed in with Apple, the app asks you to confirm with Apple first, and we then ask Apple to revoke the sign-in link between PrepMe and your Apple ID. If you cancel that confirmation your account is kept; if Apple cannot be reached the account is still deleted, only without the revocation.

It does not cancel your subscription. Subscriptions continue to bill until you cancel them with Apple or Google Play (how). The app tells you this and offers a Manage subscription button before you confirm the deletion.

Deleting your account also does not remove data held by Apple, RevenueCat, Google or Anthropic under their own policies, analytics data or crash reports until Google expires them.

Export your data

In the app, open Profile → Privacy & receipt photos and choose the export option to save the data we hold about you as a file (JSON): your account details, preferences, allergies, household members, AI consent record, pantry, follow-up requests, recipes, cooking and scan history, usage counts, devices and subscription record. Scan photos are not included because they are never kept.

Signing out

Signing out removes the copy of your pantry, recipes, follow-ups, allergies and consent that the app keeps on that phone, so the next person who signs in sees none of it. Your data stays in your account and comes back when you sign in again.

Other requests

To correct data, ask a question, or exercise a right you have under applicable law, write to prepme.business@gmail.com. We will answer within a reasonable time and may need to confirm that the request comes from you.

8. Egyptian law

Egypt’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020 gives individuals rights over their personal data, including to know what is held, to correct it, and to withdraw consent. We take those principles into account and will handle requests made under that law or other applicable law. This policy is a description of our practices and is not a statement that PrepMe meets every requirement of any specific law.

9. International transfers

Our main database is in the EU (Ireland). Our own AI server is hosted by Hostinger in Germany (EU). Anthropic and some other processors listed above handle data in other countries, including the United States, which can have different data-protection rules from Egypt. The app’s AI consent screen asks for your agreement to these transfers before your first AI or scan request.

10. Security

Connections use encryption (HTTPS). Each account’s data is separated by access rules so that you can read only your own data, and scan photos are in private storage. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not store anything highly sensitive in the app.

If a data breach is likely to put users at risk, we will notify Egypt’s Personal Data Protection Centre (PDPC) within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, and the affected users without undue delay after that.

11. Children

PrepMe is for people aged 18 and over. It is not directed to anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe someone under 18 has given us data, write to prepme.business@gmail.com and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we make a material change we will update the effective date above and, where the law requires it, tell you in the app. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
