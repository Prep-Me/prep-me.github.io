Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

This policy explains what the PrepMe apps for iPhone and Android (“PrepMe”, “the app”) collect, why, who else handles it, how long we keep it, and what control you have. PrepMe (“we”, “us”) is the publisher and is responsible for your data. Contact: prepme.business@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You sign in with Apple or Google. We keep your email and name, plus what you add: pantry, chats, recipes, allergies and preferences.
  • Before you first use the AI and scan features, the app asks you to agree to how your data is sent to our AI providers (section 4). We keep a record of your agreement.
  • Receipt and grocery photos are uploaded and read by AI, and then deleted once the result is saved.
  • Your recipe requests and scan photos pass through our own AI server and go to one third-party AI provider at a time (Anthropic first, then Google, then Groq). One of them, Google’s Gemini API, runs on a free tier, where the provider may use that content to improve its products and human reviewers may read it. Do not put sensitive personal information in chats.
  • No ads. No tracking across other companies’ apps or sites. We do not sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and export your data inside the app.

2. AI recipes and chat

The app asks for your agreement before the first AI or scan request and does not send anything to these providers until you agree. You can withdraw your agreement by deleting your account or by writing to us; without it the AI and scan features cannot be used.

To write recipes and answer follow-ups, our server passes the request to our own AI service, which runs on a server we rent in Germany and stores nothing. It sends the request to one AI provider at a time, in this order, moving to the next only if one is unavailable, too slow or gives an unusable answer: Anthropic (Claude) first, then the Google Gemini API, then Groq (details in section 4). Receipt and grocery photos follow the same order (section 3). The request can contain your pantry items, your preferences, the foods to avoid, and your chat messages. We do not send your name, email, account ID, household members’ names, or the fact that a food is an allergy to any of them.

Listing a provider means we may send your data to it. While AI features are being switched on, some or all of them may at times answer with built-in sample content and send nothing to any provider.

Anthropic does not use API requests to train its models and keeps them only for a limited period under its terms. Our access to the Gemini API is on its free tier: under Google’s terms for that tier, Google may use prompts and responses to improve its products and services, and human reviewers may read them, and this applies to any scan photo that reaches Google too. Groq keeps data under its own terms (section 4). For this reason, please do not enter sensitive personal information (health records, government ID numbers, financial details, other people’s private data) into chats or photos. AI output can be inaccurate; see the Terms.

We store the result in your account. Separately, we log technical details of each AI call (which feature, model, token counts, time taken, estimated cost and whether it succeeded) for cost control and debugging. These logs do not contain your prompts or the AI’s answers. Our AI service also records a technical trace of each request (the steps taken, timings, models, token counts and errors) in a monitoring tool that runs on our own server; it does not contain your pantry, messages, recipes or photos.

3. Receipt and grocery photos

  1. The app shrinks your photo and uploads it to a private storage area that only your account and our server can reach.
  2. Our server passes the photo to our AI service, which sends it to one AI provider that reads the items from it: Anthropic (Claude) first, then the Google Gemini API, then Groq if the others are unavailable. There is no separate text-reading step. If none of them can read it, the scan does not complete and you can add the items by hand.
  3. The items it returns are shown to you to review before they are added to your pantry.
  4. The photo is deleted from our storage as soon as the result is saved. We keep the list of items, not the picture.

If a scan fails or you leave halfway, the uploaded photo may stay in storage until we remove it; it is always deleted with your account. The AI provider that read the photo handles it under its own terms and retention rules (sections 4 and 5). Receipts can show names or partial card numbers; crop them out before scanning if you prefer.

5. No ads, no tracking, no sale

PrepMe shows no ads and contains no advertising or cross-app tracking. We do not sell or rent your personal data and we do not share it for advertising. We may disclose data if the law requires it or to protect our rights and users’ safety.

Third-party services keep some data under their own rules, for example the AI providers for the requests they receive, and Apple or Google Play for purchases. Deleting your PrepMe account does not delete data those companies hold under their own policies; ask them directly if you want that.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account data (profile, allergies, AI consent record, pantry, chats, recipes, cooking history, scan results, devices, usage events, feedback you sent, subscription record): until you delete your account. Items you delete in the app are marked deleted and removed with your account.
  • Scan photos: deleted right after the scan result is saved; any leftover is deleted with your account.
  • AI call logs and monitoring traces: technical details only, kept for cost, abuse and error monitoring.
  • Usage analytics: kept by Google Analytics for a limited period under our settings. They are not linked to your PrepMe account, so deleting the account does not remove them.
  • Install ID and free-use counters: the app sends a random ID created for this installation (it is not your name, email, advertising ID or phone number). We store it with this month’s free-use counts and which account first used the welcome days on it, so that a new account on the same phone does not get a fresh free allowance. This record has no link to your account and is kept after you delete your account, for abuse prevention: usage counts for 3 months, the welcome-days record for 12 months.
  • Crash reports: kept by Firebase for a limited period under its settings.
  • Billing records: kept by Apple or Google Play, and RevenueCat, under their policies and the law.
  • Backups and logs: copies may persist for a short period after deletion before they are overwritten.

7. Your choices

Delete your account

In the app, open Profile → Privacy & receipt photos → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account and the data we hold for it: profile and preferences, allergies, AI consent record, pantry, chats and recipes, cooking history, scan results and any stored scan photos, household members, feedback, devices, usage events and our subscription record. You can also delete your account without the app.

If you signed in with Apple, the app asks you to confirm with Apple first, and we then ask Apple to revoke the sign-in link between PrepMe and your Apple ID. If you cancel that confirmation your account is kept; if Apple cannot be reached the account is still deleted, only without the revocation.

It does not cancel your subscription. Subscriptions continue to bill until you cancel them with Apple or Google Play (how). The app tells you this and offers a Manage subscription button before you confirm the deletion.

Deleting your account also does not remove data held by Apple, RevenueCat, Anthropic, Google or Groq under their own policies, analytics data or crash reports until Google expires them.

Export your data

In the app, open Profile → Privacy & receipt photos and choose the export option to save the data we hold about you as a file (JSON): your account details, preferences, allergies, household members, AI consent record, pantry, chats, recipes, cooking and scan history, usage counts, feedback, devices and subscription record. Scan photos are not included because they are deleted after reading.

Signing out

Signing out removes the copy of your pantry, recipes, chats, allergies and consent that the app keeps on that phone, so the next person who signs in sees none of it. Your data stays in your account and comes back when you sign in again.

Other requests

To correct data, ask a question, or exercise a right you have under applicable law, write to prepme.business@gmail.com. We will answer within a reasonable time and may need to confirm that the request comes from you.

8. Egyptian law

Egypt’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020 gives individuals rights over their personal data, including to know what is held, to correct it, and to withdraw consent. We take those principles into account and will handle requests made under that law or other applicable law. This policy is a description of our practices and is not a statement that PrepMe meets every requirement of any specific law.

9. International transfers

Our main database is in the EU (Ireland) and our AI service runs in the EU (Germany). Some processors listed above may handle data in other countries, including the United States and Singapore, which can have different data-protection rules from Egypt. By using the AI and scan features you understand that this data goes to those services.

10. Security

Connections use encryption (HTTPS). Each account’s data is separated by access rules so that you can read only your own data, and scan photos are in private storage. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not store anything highly sensitive in the app.

11. Children

PrepMe is for people aged 18 and over. It is not directed to anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe someone under 18 has given us data, write to prepme.business@gmail.com and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we make a material change we will update the effective date above and, where the law requires it, tell you in the app. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
